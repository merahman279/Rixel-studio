FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding contracts, timelines, source files, and payments.

Turnaround depends on project scope. Brand identity concepts are delivered within 3 to 5 business days. Social media ad packs take 48 to 72 hours. Video edits and complex motion graphics typically require 4 to 7 business days. Rush delivery (24-48 hours) is available upon request.
I prioritize 100% client satisfaction through structured feedback loops. Every project includes dedicated iterative revision cycles where we fine-tune colors, fonts, timing, and micro-details until the work is flawless.
You receive complete, unlocked source files: Adobe Illustrator (.AI, .EPS), Photoshop (.PSD), After Effects (.AEP), Premiere Pro (.PRPROJ), and Figma links. Exports include print-ready CMYK PDFs, high-res PNG/SVG web assets, and 4K ProRes/MP4 video renders.
For Bangladeshi clients, payments are accepted in BDT (৳) via bKash, Nagad, and direct local Bank Transfers. For international partners, payments are processed in USD ($) via Payoneer, Wise, and International Wire Transfer. A 50% deposit begins the project.
